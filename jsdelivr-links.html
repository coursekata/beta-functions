Playground Link Maker

Paste a link to a Jupyter notebook on GitHub. You'll get CourseKata Playground links that load the notebook through jsDelivr, so they work even at schools that block GitHub.

The repository must be public. Links from github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com or jsDelivr all work.
